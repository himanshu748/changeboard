Follow the venue change

Use a fresh browser profile if you already have an active workspace. Leave the Sample workspace label visible.

  1. Open Try the sample campaign. Select the three scheduled posts, then Review facts.
  2. Keep the original facts. Choose Confirm current facts, then review and confirm the six suggested exact phrases.
  3. Under The new plan, change only Venue name to River Studio. Keep November 21 and 18:00 unchanged. Choose Preview changes.
  4. Compare the full captions. On the first post, choose Use updated event poster, inspect it and acknowledge its accuracy for this revision. Leave the second image unresolved.
  5. On the third post, open Test an external edit and choose Simulate edit in Buffer. Its changed source is withheld.
  6. Check that one post is eligible. Choose Simulate updates to 1 post. Inspect one verified sample result and two skips. The skipped schedules remain unchanged.
  7. Download the repair report. Try another venue change to inspect retained associations and renewed image review.

These outcomes are simulated. They demonstrate review, image warnings and conflict handling; they are separate from the live API evidence.
